Neb0Font design rules

glyphs are drawn in a 3x5 grid, which gives a 4x6 repeating cell with blank space
counting from top left:
row 5 is for the 'descenders'
capital letters and numbers fill rows 1-4, with Q also using row 5
lower case letters fill either rows 2-4 or 3-4;
3-4 is preferred, with 'tall' letters also using row 2, but not always possible (c, f, x, g)
dotted letters (i, j) put the dot in row 1, leave row 2 blank, and start the stem in row 3
generally try to avoid shapes composed entirely of isolated pixels, but not always possible (lower case x, /, \, *)
usually break the top corner of a shape that is too 'square' (A, C, G, Q)
!, ?, &, % are allowed to use row 5, though they should sit on the line
1 column letters are centred, and 2 column letters are left aligned
